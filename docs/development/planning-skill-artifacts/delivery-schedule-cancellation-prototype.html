Delivery schedule cancellation

This demo asks: can dispatch coordinators understand a Delivery schedule with the three progression stages draft, confirmed, and dispatched when it may be cancelled only before dispatch?

Verdict: Yes, if cancellation is shown as a separate, terminal operational outcome while the three stages retain the last reached scheduling stage. A cancelled confirmed schedule must visibly say both “confirmed” and “cancelled”; it must not appear available for dispatch.
