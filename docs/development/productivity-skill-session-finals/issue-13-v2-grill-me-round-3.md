❓ **Q5** - **Threshold treatment**: Once an Incident card reaches 15 minutes without an update, should it receive a persistent, explicit visual marker in addition to its position, or should position alone indicate attention?

➡️ I recommend an **explicit “Needs attention” marker plus the elapsed time**. Position identifies the first card; the marker makes the condition scannable across the board.

---

❓ **Q6** - **Equal-age cards**: When multiple Incident cards have the same last-updated time, should they share the same priority position or use Owner as a deterministic tie-breaker?

➡️ I recommend **treating them as equal priority**, displayed together. Alphabetical Owner ordering may look meaningful even though it has no operational significance.

---

❓ **Q7** - **Time progression**: Should the board automatically re-evaluate and reorder cards as time passes, including when a card crosses the 15-minute threshold, or only when an Incident card is updated?

➡️ I recommend **automatic re-evaluation**. A wall display should remain truthful without requiring an operator to refresh it; this is display behavior, not a notification.
