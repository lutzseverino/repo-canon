❓ **Q11** - **Threshold boundary**: Does an Incident card become “Needs attention” exactly at 15 minutes since its last update, or only after it exceeds 15 minutes?

➡️ I recommend **exactly at 15 minutes**. It makes the rule unambiguous and matches “after 15 minutes without an update.”

---

❓ **Q12** - **Update response**: When an Incident card receives a new update, should the board immediately recalculate its elapsed time, remove its marker if applicable, and reposition it, or wait for the next one-minute re-evaluation?

➡️ I recommend **immediately recalculate and reposition it**. The wall display should reflect new incident activity as soon as it is known; the one-minute cadence covers time passing between updates.

---

❓ **Q13** - **Owner context**: Should every Incident card show its Owner alongside the elapsed time, while keeping Owner out of the attention ranking?

➡️ I recommend **show the Owner without using it to rank**. After identifying the card that needs attention first, operators can immediately see who is accountable for its next action.
