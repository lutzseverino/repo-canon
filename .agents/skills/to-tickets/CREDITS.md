# Credits

This skill is Repo Canon's own, taken from the `to-tickets` skill in [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills). Its upstream base is tag `v1.3.1`, commit `24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888`, at [`skills/engineering/to-tickets`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888/skills/engineering/to-tickets). That material is used under its MIT License, whose notice adoption installs beside the skills as `.agents/skills/LICENSE.mattpocock-skills`.

The skill differs from its upstream base as follows. `SKILL.md` states that a prompt or brief naming the skill is its invocation. It also drops both routes to `setup-matt-pocock-skills`. Expand–contract batches that cannot each stay green become one ticket instead of sharing an integration branch, since every ticket merges on its own with its checks passing. On a real tracker, parent and blocker links are native only, and a link that cannot be created is reported instead of written into the body. Tickets are published awaiting an independent readiness review instead of carrying `ready-for-agent`, and the local ticket template's status is `needs-triage`.
