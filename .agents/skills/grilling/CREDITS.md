# Credits

This skill is Repo Canon's own, taken from the `grilling` skill in [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills). Its upstream base is tag `v1.3.1`, commit `24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888`, at [`skills/productivity/grilling`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888/skills/productivity/grilling). That material is used under its MIT License, whose notice follows at the end of this file; adoption also installs it beside the skills. The skill differs from its upstream base: `SKILL.md` states that a prompt or brief naming the skill is its invocation. It also asks the frontier in rounds and keeps each round short, asking the frontier questions that unblock the most first when the frontier holds more than the user can weigh at once. It reuses recorded dated answers, selects each round’s decision maker through the planning rules, and retains grill-answer and plan-approval gates without an extra quiz. It may build a throwaway prototype with the `prototype` skill when words cannot settle a look or how a state model feels, and a grill that adds or changes a mechanism or policy ends with an independent review against long-term design, asked as its final round. A composite skill continues to publication after the interview; its Reads section supplies the contribution-guide fallback. Planning policy is read from the target repository’s installed planning rules, with its contribution guide only as the non-adopter fallback. Both standalone and composite grills identify the maintainer or delegated agent as their other party. Its publishing final round combines the existing strategic review with the specification repository, ticket split, repositories, blockers, testing seams and publication go-ahead, reuses applicable recorded approval and reopens when required questions return.
