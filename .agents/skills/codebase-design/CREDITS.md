This skill is Repo Canon's own, taken from the `codebase-design` skill in [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills). Its upstream base is tag `v1.3.1`, commit `24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888`, at [`skills/engineering/codebase-design`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888/skills/engineering/codebase-design). That material is used under its MIT License, whose notice follows at the end of this file; adoption also installs it beside the skills. The skill differs from its upstream base: `SKILL.md` states that a prompt or brief naming the skill is its invocation. It adds a Reads section, which lists the repository files the skill reads and what it does when each is missing. It adds the shared `OWNERSHIP.md` branch reference: value owners, triggers and cost read, wrapping and extraction defaults, deferred seams, narrow touched-code cleanup, contract disagreement routing, and capability/policy and vendored UI examples. `SKILL.md` links that branch and retains the common design vocabulary and testing method; its redundant Relationships recap is removed. The Reads section declares the reference’s repository inputs. Planning policy is read from the target repository’s installed planning rules, with its contribution guide only as the non-adopter fallback. In `OWNERSHIP.md`, proposed fixes follow the checkable-fix and refactor-first order before ownership routes, including a no-trigger result, retaining contract and scope limits.
