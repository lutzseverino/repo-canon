# Planning rules adoption guidance

Confirm an intentionally empty scope for `planning`: no content paths or
directories. Adoption installs its rules for the whole repository.

Reconcile existing triage, readiness, findings and question practices with the
installed planning rules, including decision rights, independent contract
acceptance and exact-revision readiness. Adoption replaces the exact
contribution guide with role pointers; preserve repository-specific constraints
through `project-agent-guidance` instead of copying planning policy back.

Assess `planning` as `satisfied` when the installed rules and repository
practice agree, citing reviewed contracts or the absence of planning activity.
Assess an unresolved conflict as `blocked` with its evidence. Confirm scope
validity after fixes and after contextual work: it remains intentionally empty.
